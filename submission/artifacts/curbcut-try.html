    What you just used

    The ranker is not a model. It stems what you typed, drops stopwords, and scores each row by how much a word distinguishes it. A word that appears across half the library carries almost no signal, which is why "something in the office" returns nothing rather than guessing at a chair.

    Control words never reach a model either. They resolve before any inference, so a withdrawal cannot depend on a model having a good day.
