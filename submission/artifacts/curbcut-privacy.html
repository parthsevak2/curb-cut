Curb Cut · Privacy Policy

What we hold, and what does not exist.

The shortest way to protect information is not to collect it. Most of this policy is about what Curb Cut deliberately has no place to store.

Who runs this

Curb Cut is operated by Parth Sevak, a sole proprietor in Welland, Ontario, Canada. Questions about this policy, or about data held about you, go to parth.sevak2@gmail.com.

Information that does not exist in this system

There is no field anywhere in Curb Cut for a diagnosis, condition, disability type, medical note, severity or prognosis.

Not encrypted, not permission-restricted, absent. This is enforced by the database schema and by an automated check that fails the build if anyone adds such a field.

If you tell the agent about a medical condition anyway, it is taken out of the text before anything is saved, and you are told so in the same reply. What is kept is the part about what is hard, which is the part we work from.

We will not overstate this. That removal runs against a list of conditions we recognise, and no list covers every word in every language. It is a real safeguard, not a guarantee, and you deserve to know which. What is guaranteed is that nothing here is built to hold a condition, so none is ever asked for, indexed, reported on, or shown to your employer.

Words like Deaf, blind and hard of hearing are deliberately left alone. They are how people describe themselves and what they need, and the interpreter routing depends on reading them.

What we do collect

WhatWhy
A one-way hash of your phone number To keep your conversation continuous across messages. The raw number is hashed with a secret salt the moment it arrives and is never written to storage or to logs.
What you say is hard at work, in your own words To look up what adjustments might help. Recorded as a functional description, what is difficult, never why it is difficult.
Standing preferences you ask us to remember So you do not have to explain the same thing to every new manager. Stored in your exact words, with who you chose to share it with.
Requests you explicitly approve To send the request you approved to your employer, and to track the response.
A record of every time a preference was shown to someone So you can see it. A preference that travels ahead of you is a disclosure made in your absence, and you are entitled to know.

Conversations are anonymous by default. No identity is attached unless you choose to attach one.

What we never do

We do not sell your information. Not to anyone, at any price, ever.
We do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. No data brokers, no advertisers, no analytics networks, no partners.
We do not use it for marketing. No promotional messages are sent through this service at all. Every message you receive is a direct reply to a message you sent.
We do not infer anything about you from your behaviour. Nothing is profiled, scored or predicted. Every signal in the system is one you set, in your own words.
We do not send anything to your employer without your explicit approval in the conversation, given at the time.

Service providers

Two companies process data on our behalf, under contract, solely to make the service work. Neither may use your information for their own purposes.

  • Twilio Inc. delivers the text messages and phone calls. Twilio handles your phone number in order to route messages, as any carrier must.
  • Salesforce, Inc. hosts the database and the agent.

Data is stored in Salesforce's United States infrastructure. We disclose information to no one else, except where law compels it, and there is no medical or diagnostic information in the system to disclose.

Your control

  • Send HUMAN at any time and a real person takes over, on whichever channel you are already using. You will not have to explain it again, and nobody will ring you.
  • Send WHO with your code, like WHO 4KQ7MT , to see everyone who has been shown a standing preference of yours.
  • Send OFF with your code to turn a standing preference off. It takes effect immediately, and you will not be asked why. The system has nowhere to record a reason, so no one can ask you for one later.
  • Reply DELETE to discard a drafted request before it is sent.
  • Reply STOP to stop receiving messages from this number entirely.
  • Email parth.sevak2@gmail.com to ask what is held about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted.

When you save a standing preference we give you six characters and show them once. We keep only a scrambled version that cannot be turned back into the code. That is the price of not holding a file on you: there is no account here, no password and no number on record, so there is nothing we could look you up by, and the code is what lets you switch a disclosure off from a text message without us ever knowing who you are. If you lose it, send HUMAN and a person will sort it out.

Records are kept only while the request or preference they support is live. An anonymous conversation that produces no request leaves no identifiable record at all.
