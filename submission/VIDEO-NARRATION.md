# Curb Cut, demo film. Narration script

Run time **4:58**. Picture is locked, and these timings are generated from the
same table that cuts the film, so they cannot drift apart from it.

**The film already carries its own captions and works in silence.** That is a
choice, not a shortcut: a film about access should not assume you can hear it.
If you do record a voice over it, keep it low and unhurried. The pictures are
making the argument already, and a narrator pushing the feeling will undo it.

Every line below fits its hold at 140 words per minute with two seconds of air.
Where a card already carries its sentence, the direction is silence: reading a
slide aloud to somebody who is reading it is how a film loses them.
