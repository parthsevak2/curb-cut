Curb Cut turns asking into one sentence. You say what's hard, in your own words, and you never have to say why. It shows you what other people in your situation have asked for, from a library of real accommodations, each with its source and what it usually costs. Knowing is allowed, and asking is optional. If you do want to ask, it writes the request in your words, reads it back, and sends nothing until you clearly say yes.

There's no field for a diagnosis anywhere. It isn't encrypted or hidden behind a permission. It simply isn't there, and a build check fails if anyone adds one. Name a condition anyway and it's stripped before anything is stored, and you're told so.

Six doors, one path, all live today. An Agentforce agent written in Agent Script decides what to say. A deterministic layer decides what may be true: it ranks the library, strips conditions, and holds the consent gate. So the answer is the same whichever door you use, and the parts that keep somebody safe never depend on a model remembering a rule.
