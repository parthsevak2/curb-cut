One in four adults has a disability. In 2025, fewer than one in four Americans with a disability was employed, and the ones who work are twice as likely to be part-time and more often in service, production and transport jobs: the shift worker, the cleaner, the minimum-wage hire with no work email and no HR desk. Across 655 large employers in 2025, the median share of staff who told their employer was 3.5%, and it didn't rise. Most decide asking isn't worth it and carry on hurting quietly.

The form is the barrier. To get help you must find the policy, learn its words, hold a work login, and explain your body to the person who decides what you're worth. For someone Deaf, or with a condition nobody can see, that form is a door that doesn't open.

The money was never the barrier: 61% of 1,425 employers who gave the Job Accommodation Network cost figures through 2024 said the accommodation cost nothing.
