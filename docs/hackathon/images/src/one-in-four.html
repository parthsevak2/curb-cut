One in four adults has a disability. About three in a hundred have told their employer.

The people in between manage quietly. They work around it, they leave early and make the time up, they carry the pain home rather than explain it to a manager.

Curb Cut was built for them: a way to find out what could help, and to ask for it in your own words, without saying why.
